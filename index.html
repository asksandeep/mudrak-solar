FAQ

Questions homeowners ask

Under the central PM Surya Ghar scheme, eligible households receive ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000. Rules can change, so we confirm current terms during your survey.

A 1 kW system typically generates 4 to 4.5 units a day, about 120 to 135 units a month. Actual output depends on shade, tilt and cleaning.

Match it to your monthly bill. Up to ₹2,000 suits 1 to 2 kW, ₹2,000 to ₹3,500 suits 2 to 3 kW, and ₹5,000 or more usually needs 5 kW or above. The survey confirms the exact size.

Net metering sends your extra solar power back to the grid. Your meter records what you export and your bill is credited for it. We assist with the full application.

Panels still generate on cloudy days, at roughly 25 to 40 percent of peak. On-grid systems switch off during a cut for safety. For backup power, choose a hybrid system with a battery.
